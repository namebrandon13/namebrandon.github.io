The idea

A short, replayable arcade game about tight movement and quick reactions. You fly through an obstacle-filled patch of space, avoid enemies and collect energy cores.

What I built

  • Real-time physics for movement and collisions
  • Wave-based enemy spawners that set the pace of each run
  • Object pooling, so spawning lots of enemies and hazards doesn't cause hitches
  • Procedurally generated hazards, so each run plays out differently
  • A HUD with a timer and energy bar, and a pause menu

What I focused on

System design, tuning the player feedback, gameplay pacing through the wave-based enemy logic, and performance.
