The idea

TUTOR.EXE is a cheerful program called Shellby that teaches you the command line using real commands, in real folders on your PC. It was built to keep students engaged. It never let the last four leave.

Their logs are still on the disk, half erased. You find out what happened to them, and use everything Shellby taught you to get out.

What's in it

  • Notepad and File Explorer open on their own, and the window and your mouse fight back
  • A tutor that notices when you look away
  • You actually learn the command line while playing
  • It only touches its own folder next to the .exe

How I made it

This one was an experiment in a different way of working: I built it with Claude as an AI pair programmer in Godot 4. It's a proof of concept and I'd welcome feedback. The itch.io page discloses AI assistance for code, graphics, sounds and text.

heads-up: it reopens itself if you close it. Task Manager gets you out
