Coach Number 11

solo · Unreal Engine · horror puzzle · Windows · prototype

You need to reach coach 11. Walk through each coach, decide whether it's the same as the last one or something has changed, and choose. The idea came from Shinkansen 0 and The Exit 8; mine is set in an Indian railway sleeper coach.

I made it on my own, including every 3D model, in Blender.

lesson learned: design all the anomalies before writing code

VR Rage Room

solo · Unity · VR · MSc dissertation · 2024

My MSc dissertation: a rage room you can play in VR or on a flat screen, built to compare how people feel after each. There are six zones: slice things with a sword, dent a soft-body punching bag, shatter plates, knock down a stack of boxes, throw axes, or do all of it at once in the Chaos zone. I made it on my own, every 3D model included.

The hardest part was getting the physics right while holding a stable 90 FPS on mid-tier hardware, which I got to with custom collision meshes.

Cosmic Chaos

solo · Unity · arcade space shooter · Windows & Linux · released

A fast arcade game from an academic project: fly through a messy patch of space, dodge enemies, grab energy cores. The hazards are procedurally generated so each run plays out differently.

Under the hood it's real-time physics, object pooling and wave-based enemy spawners, tuned until the pacing felt right.
